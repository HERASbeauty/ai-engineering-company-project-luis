# TrackFlow Design System

**Brand:** TrackFlow — Immersive Premium\
**Style:** Hybrid Cinematic + Digital\
**Status:** Approved V1.0

## Visual direction

The visual language is modern, premium, cinematic, and professional. It combines authentic physical logistics environments with digital visual storytelling. Alternate dark, immersive sections with light, editorial sections.

## Approved hero

Use the approved desktop and mobile compositions without changing their visual direction:

- A cinematic warehouse environment with packages on a prominent conveyor belt.
- A large white headline with cyan emphasis.
- A digital map highlighting the United States and Spain, connected by thin blue/cyan lines.
- Small glowing particles that move subtly along the routes to suggest digital transactions or package movement.
- Gentle pulses at destinations.
- A prominent **Request Information** call to action.
- Preserve the approved desktop and mobile compositions shown in the reference asset: [trackflow-approved-hero-v1.png](./trackflow-approved-hero-v1.png).

## Motion

- Route particles travel approximately every 6–8 seconds.
- Use smooth text reveals and section transitions.
- Keep hover effects subtle.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain mobile performance and readability.

## Design principles and product constraints

- Keep the experience modern, premium, cinematic, and professional.
- Balance physical logistics imagery with restrained digital visual storytelling.
- Alternate immersive dark sections with light editorial sections.
- Do not imply real-time tracking or other operational capabilities that are unverified.
- Preserve all requirements in the official [Milestone 1 briefing](../milestone-1/BRIEFING.md).
