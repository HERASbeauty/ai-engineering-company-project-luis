# TrackFlow Stage 2 Design

**Status:** Approved V1.0

## Purpose and reference

This document records the approved visual direction for the Stage 2 landing-page sections. The approved desktop and mobile mockup is preserved at [trackflow-stage2-approved-v1.png](./trackflow-stage2-approved-v1.png).

This is a design specification only. It does not declare that any new website sections, integrations, or operational capabilities have been implemented.

## Design system

Reuse the approved colors, typography, and motion principles in the [TrackFlow Design System](./TRACKFLOW_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md):

- Midnight Navy `#071422`, Deep Ocean `#10283E`, Signal Blue `#1D67F2`, Flow Cyan `#62E3DF`, Cloud White `#F3F6F8`, and Steel Mist `#9EB3C5`.
- Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for body text.
- Keep interactions subtle, support `prefers-reduced-motion`, and preserve readability across screen sizes.

## Services

- Use a light editorial background and three premium photographic cards for Warehousing, Last-mile Delivery, and Reverse Logistics.
- Include cyan outlined service icons, concise descriptions, and subtle interactions.
- Present the cards in a three-column desktop layout and stack them vertically on mobile.
- Keep card imagery and copy focused on the named services; photography is not evidence of a specific system, integration, or service capability.

## Coverage

- Use a dark navy section with a dotted geographic map emphasizing the United States and Spain.
- Identify Los Angeles and Zaragoza clearly, with elegant cyan connections between the locations.
- Treat the map and connections as illustrative geography, not live tracking, shipment status, or operational telemetry.
- Keep the visualization legible and restrained so the location labels remain clear.

## Why TrackFlow

- Use a clean white editorial section with four concise business-benefit blocks.
- Pair cyan icons with clear typography and generous spacing.
- Support every public-facing claim with the official [Milestone 1 briefing](../milestone-1/BRIEFING.md) and the project’s operational-data guardrails. The mockup is a visual reference, not an authoritative source of business claims.
- Do not present exclusivity, guaranteed delivery performance, live tracking, real-time inventory visibility, or proprietary technology as verified capabilities unless separately confirmed by an authoritative source.

## Contact

- Use a cinematic closing section with a prominent **Request information** call to action linking to `/application.html`.
- Preserve the required business contact information elsewhere on the page: `comercial@trackflow.com`, Los Angeles `+1 213 555 0147`, and Zaragoza `+34 976 123 456`.
- Keep the CTA and contact information readable and accessible against the photographic background.

## Mobile and accessibility

- Follow the approved mobile composition, stacking the service cards vertically.
- Keep the coverage visualization readable; simplify its presentation when needed without implying live operational data.
- Prevent horizontal overflow and maintain accessible contrast, visible keyboard focus, and usable tap targets.
- Preserve the Stage 1 reduced-motion behavior and avoid motion or effects that compete with content.

## Content integrity

The mockup guides visual composition only. Verify all future copy against authoritative project sources, distinguish documented facts from planned capabilities, and do not infer operational performance or integrations from the visual design.
